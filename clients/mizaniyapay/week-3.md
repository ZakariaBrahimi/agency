## Tue 17 Nov · Real-people reel: shop-owner story · Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn

To film with a real Mizaniya Pay partner merchant. End on `03-tue-shop-owner-outro.mp4` (3.5 s, partner link).

**Format:** 25–40 s, the owner talks to camera in their shop, with cut-aways of a customer paying by QR.

Questions to ask (keep answers short and natural, in Darija):

1. «واش تبيع، ومن قداش راك في الحانوت؟» — What do you sell, and since when?
2. «قبل، واش كان المشكل مع الخلاص؟» — Before, what was the problem with payments? (no change, "I'll pay you tomorrow", no TPE)
3. «كيفاش بديت مع Mizaniya Pay؟» — How did you start?
4. «واش تبدّل من بعد؟» — What changed since?
5. «واش تقول لتاجر آخر؟» — What would you tell another shop owner?

Shot list:

| Shot | What we see |
| --- | --- |
| Opening (0–3 s) | Owner's best line from answer 2 or 4, straight to camera |
| B-roll | Shop front, the QR code on the counter, a customer scanning and paying |
| Middle | Answers 2–4, cut tight |
| Close (last line) | Answer 5, then the outro card |

On-screen hook (first 2 s): «منين ولّيت بالـQR، ما بقاش "نخلصك غدوة"» — "Since QR, no more 'I'll pay you tomorrow'". Only use this line if the owner says something close to it; otherwise use their own words.

Needs: the merchant's signed image consent, the shop's name only if they agree, and no real revenue figures on screen.

> «منين ولّيت بالـQR…» 🏪 [Shop name] تحكيلنا كيفاش تبدّل الخلاص في الحانوت.
