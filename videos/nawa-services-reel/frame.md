# Nawa · frame.md

Brand truth for video. Arabic-first, right to left.

## Colors (strict)
- Ink `#0B1220` (background, every scene)
- Indigo `#5B4BFF` (brand, primary surfaces)
- Lime `#C6F432` (the single accent: highlights, the core dot, success)
- Paper `#F6F5F2` (light cards)
- Text `#F1F2F6`; muted text `#A3ABC2`; panels `#151D31` / `#1D2741`; lines `#2A3553`

## Type
- Arabic: IBM Plex Sans Arabic 700 (headlines), 500 (body). Never letter-space Arabic.
- Latin display: Bricolage Grotesque 800 (numbers, English words).
- Labels: JetBrains Mono 600, uppercase, tracked.

## Mark
The letter ن as an open white bowl with a lime dot (the "core") on an indigo rounded square.

## Do / Don't
- Do keep lime for the one thing that matters in a frame.
- Don't use gradients across the full frame; use radial glows.
- Don't invent claims: figures are labelled illustrative.
